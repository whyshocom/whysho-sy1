whysho · synthesizer

SY1 is installed

Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Look for whysho SY1 among your instruments and load it on a software-instrument or MIDI track; it makes sound when it receives notes.

To unlock it, open the license panel inside SY1 and sign in with your whysho account. Without one, a 14-day trial starts by itself.
